  abstract.en={This work deals with a component of encryption algorithms called the S-box (substitution box) and its evolutionary design.
  Evolutionary computation techniques are used for its development, including the classical genetic algorithm,
  Estimation of Distribution Algorithm, Cartesian Genetic Programming, and multi-criteria
  algorithms VEGA and SPEA. The aim of this thesis is to explore the properties of substitution boxes for their evolutionary development.
  The work first addresses cryptography and the issues surrounding S-boxes. Basic
  concepts are explained and selected security criteria are described. The evolutionary algorithms used
  and fundamentals of multi-criteria optimization are then presented. This knowledge is applied to the design and implementation of the program,
  which are described in subsequent chapters. Finally, the application of the studied criteria is discussed, including the search for
  S-boxes in both single-criterion and, particularly, multi-criteria genetic search.
