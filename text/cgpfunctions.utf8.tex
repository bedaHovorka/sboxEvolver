This appendix explains the notation in the Cartesian genetic programming chromosome.
The notation was used in accordance with the CGPTools tool \cite{vasicek}.
\subsubsection{Chromosome Format}
The header contains data in this order:
\begin{verbatim}
  {number of inputs, number of outputs, columns, rows, number of inputs to block, l-back,
   number of blocks used}
\end{verbatim}
This is followed by descriptions of individual blocks (the input of the entire circuit is also considered an input block; blocks
are numbered from the number of inputs):
\begin{verbatim}
  ([block number] input block a, input block b, function)
\end{verbatim}
Finally, the outputs of some blocks (specified numbers) are declared as outputs of the entire circuit:
\begin{verbatim}
  (list of blocks)
\end{verbatim}


\subsubsection{Function Assignment}
For input variables $a$ and $b$:

\begin{center}
     \begin{tabular}{| c | c |}
      \hline
      Function Number & Expression \\ \hline\hline
      0 & $a$ \\ \hline
      1 & $a$ AND $b$ \\ \hline
      2 & $a$ OR $b$ \\ \hline
      3 & $a$ XOR $b$ \\ \hline
      4 & NOT $a$ \\ \hline
      5 & NOT $b$ \\ \hline
      6 & $a$ AND (NOT $b$) \\ \hline
      7 & $a$ NAND $b$ \\ \hline
      8 & $a$ NOR $b$ \\ \hline
     \end{tabular}
\end{center}
